# Seller onboarding playbook: the first 20–30 real Alexandria listings

**Goal:** 20–30 genuine, published listings in Alexandria, each with good photos and an honest description.
**Rule:** every listing is a real car that the seller owns or is authorized to sell. No demo cars, no copied photos, no invented prices.

## 1. Who to ask first (in this order)

| Source | Why | How |
|---|---|---|
| People you know (family, friends, colleagues, building neighbours) who are selling now or soon | Highest trust, fastest yes | Message them directly; offer to help them list in 10 minutes |
| Small used-car showrooms (Smouha, Sidi Gaber, Miami, Sporting, Victor Emmanuel) | Each has several cars | Visit in person and show a live listing on your phone; they post under their own dealer account |
| Mechanics and service centers you trust | They know who is selling | Ask them to pass on the link. Also invite them to MOTORNA Care (`/ar/care/apply`) |
| Alexandria car-selling groups (Facebook, WhatsApp) | Sellers already looking for buyers | Reply to individual sellers privately, not with spam posts, and follow each group's rules |

## 2. What to say (short, honest)

> MOTORNA is a new car-selling site in Alexandria. Listing is free. A moderator checks the listing before it goes live, buyers message you through the site, and your number stays hidden unless you choose to show it. It takes about 10 minutes and I can help you right now.

Don't promise sales, buyer numbers, prices or speed. The site is new, so be clear that there are few buyers yet.

## 3. Ten-minute listing session (on the seller's phone)

1. Open `motorna.vercel.app/ar/sell` and create the account in the seller's own name and email, with a password **they** choose. Never create accounts for people or ask for their password.
2. **Car:** make, model and year. Trim only if they know it (Highline, M Sport…), never the engine size.
3. **Specs:** transmission, fuel, engine size (cc).
4. **Mileage and condition:** the actual odometer number.
5. **Price and location:** asking price and area.
   - **Phone:** explain that it's off by default. If they switch it on, signed-in buyers can call or WhatsApp them. Messages work either way.
6. **Photos (8 if possible):** front ¾, rear ¾, both sides, front seats, dashboard with the odometer visible, engine, tyres. Use daylight, no filters, and a clean background.
7. **Description and known defects:** service history, ownership, paint, and anything not working. Honest listings sell faster and avoid disputes.
8. **Review, then submit.** Tell them a moderator reviews it before it's published.

## 4. Your side: moderation routine

- Check **الإدارة ← مراجعة الإعلانات** at least twice a day while onboarding.
- **Approve** only when:
  - The photos are of this car.
  - The price is plausible.
  - There are no deposit requests or phone numbers in the text.
  - Make, model and year match the photos.
- **Reject with a clear, fixable reason**, for example "صورة العداد مش ظاهرة". The seller gets the reason by email once email is configured, and always in **إعلاناتي**.
- Every approval and rejection is recorded in the audit log.

## 5. Tracking sheet (one row per seller)

| Date | Seller (name) | Source | Car | Status: contacted / helped / submitted / published / sold | Photos ok? | Follow-up date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Weekly targets:
- **Week 1:** 5 published (people you know).
- **Week 2:** 10 more (2–3 showrooms).
- **Weeks 3–4:** the remaining 5–15.

## 6. Quality checklist before you approve your own outreach

- [ ] The seller listed it from their own account.
- [ ] At least 3 photos, ideally 8, all of this car.
- [ ] Odometer, price and area filled in.
- [ ] Trim isn't an engine size.
- [ ] Known defects filled in, or the seller confirmed they know of none.
- [ ] The phone-consent choice was explained, and the seller chose it themselves.

## 7. What not to do

- Don't add demo or placeholder cars to production, even "temporarily".
- Don't copy photos from other sites.
- Don't post on a seller's behalf from your own account.
- Don't quote market statistics or "X buyers viewed" numbers you don't have.
